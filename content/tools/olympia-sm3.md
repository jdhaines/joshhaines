## Overview

I ordered this typewriter from [Mr. & Mrs.
Vintage Typewriter](https://mrmrsvintagetypewriters.com/) based in the UK. I ordered it on August 3rd, 2026 and received it on August 10th. They shipped it very quickly and it was packaged securely with no damage.

This is my first typewriter and I was looking
for something of good quality that would last
me a long time. After watching the [Tom Hanks Typewriter Documentary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AWfgpL1X_oE) on YouTube as well as numerous other videos I decided the Olympia SM3 was the right choice for me. It had enough features to be useful, but not so many it would be overwhelming. It has a strong pedigree, is reliable, and was built with quality from the beginning. I believe it's from the late 1950s and built in West Germany. I chose the olive green color because it was a bit different than the standard black or gray, and I liked the look of it.

I also ordered a dust cover since my typewriter didn't have a case. I got it from
[TypewriterCovers](https://www.etsy.com/shop/TypewriterCovers?lid=f44v9b2c0r4v) on
Etsy. I chose the _Army Green_ color and had them embroider `SM3 De Luxe` on the
front to match the typewriter. It's pretty cool and should protect it from getting
dusty while sitting in my office.

The content items below were all drafted on my Olympia SM3...
